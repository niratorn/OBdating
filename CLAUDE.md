# วงล้ออายุครรภ์ (Preg Wheel web)

Web app คำนวณอายุครรภ์ (GA) และ EDC จาก LMP และอัลตราซาวด์ ใช้ได้ 2 แบบ: รายคน (หน้าคลินิก) และไฟล์ Excel/CSV หลายราย (งานวิจัย)
Owner: Niratorn (OB-GYN, Phichit Hospital). Started 2026-09-30.

## วิธีเปิดใช้
- เว็บจริง (GitHub Pages): https://niratorn.github.io/OBdating/ จาก repo public https://github.com/niratorn/OBdating

## Files (which one is real)
- `src/core.js`: ALL date and GA logic, pure functions. Clinical constants are at the top of this file.
- Trap: `parseDateText` and `parseGA` in src/core.js contain a literal U+00A0 inside `/[ \s]+/g`. An exact-text edit that retypes those lines with a normal space will not match. `\s` already covers U+00A0, so the literal can go the next time those lines change.
- `src/app.js`: UI (clinic tab, research tab, downloads). `src/body.html`, `src/styles.css`: markup and styles.
- `index.html`: GENERATED by `build.py` (standalone, SheetJS inlined). Never hand-edit.
- `dist/artifact.html`: GENERATED for the claude.ai artifact (SheetJS from cdnjs). Never hand-edit.
- `build.py`: run `python build.py` after any change in `src/`.
- `tests/core.test.js`: known-answer tests, `node tests/core.test.js` (no packages needed).
- `tests/e2e.py`: browser tests incl. mocked clock rollover, `python tests/e2e.py` (needs `playwright` and `openpyxl`).
- `vendor/`: SheetJS Community Edition 0.18.5 (Apache 2.0) and its licence.
- `fonts/`: self-hosted IBM Plex Sans Thai 400/500/600 and Trirong 500/600 (thai, latin, latin-ext subsets) from Fontsource 5.3.0, SIL OFL 1.1 licences included. `fonts/fonts.css` is inlined into index.html by build.py.
- `tests/fixtures/dates.json`: 1544 date cases computed with Python datetime (independent of our JS), incl. every day around 29 Feb.
- `deploy.bat`: publishes from the PC once Git for Windows is installed (see Deploy). `.nojekyll`, `.gitignore`, `.gitattributes`, `README.md` belong to the GitHub repo.

## Clinical rules (read the constants in src/core.js, never quote from memory)
- EDC from LMP = LMP + 280 days. Source: ACOG Committee Opinion No. 700, Methods for estimating the due date. Obstet Gynecol. 2017;129(5):e150-e154. doi:10.1097/AOG.0000000000002046 (PMID 28426621, reaffirmed 2022).
- Redating rule chosen by the user on 2026-09-30: discrepancy MORE THAN 7 days (to 13+6 wk), 14 days (14+0 to 27+6 wk), 21 days (28+0 wk on) means use the ultrasound EDC. A discrepancy equal to the threshold keeps LMP. Both directions count.
- The band is chosen by GA from LMP on the ultrasound date, following the ACOG CO 700 Table 1 footnote "*Based on LMP" (checked against 3 reproductions of the table: ANMC 2017 and 2024, University of Iowa).
- A third-trimester redate shows the ACOG caution about growth restriction (Table 1 dagger footnote).
- LMP that cannot date the pregnancy (user decision 2026-09-30, after a phone case LMP 01/09/2569 with U/S 16/08/2569 at 12+0): when the U/S date is before the LMP, or GA by LMP on the U/S date is beyond 44+6 wk, the app uses the U/S EDC and never stops. The user's reasoning: US GA exceeds LMP GA by more than a week, so U/S wins anyway. `assessDating()` sets `lmpConflict` ('US_BEFORE_LMP' or 'US_TOO_LATE'), source 'US', no band, no discrepancy, and the ACOG caution when the U/S GA itself is 28+0 or more. Note text in `LMP_CONFLICT_TEXT`: "มีการ US ก่อน LMP" (the user's words) and "GA ตาม LMP ณ วันทำ US เกิน 44+6 สัปดาห์". U/S on the LMP day and exactly 44+6 follow the normal rule. The doctor override is hidden in this state, because forcing LMP would give a nonsense "at GA".
- Clinic tab applies the rule automatically; the doctor can override to LMP or US. Research tab always applies the rule (column EDC_source). Conflict rows get EDC_source US, the note, and blank GA_LMP_at_US, EDC_US_minus_LMP_days, rule_band and rule_threshold_days; the stats tile counts them as "LMP ใช้ไม่ได้".

## Report line ("การรายงาน/บันทึก GA" box in the result card)
- Requested 2026-09-30: after GA is calculated and ultrasound data is entered, the page must write the patient-profile line. The earlier "ตัวอย่างการรายงานเคส" section (cases 1 to 5) was removed at the user's request.
- Format follows the teaching handout "การ Corrected date ทางสูติกรรม" (Elective สูติศาสตร์ ปี 4, เม.ย. 2566, ตรวจทานโดย อ.นพ.นิรทร ศรีสุโข). Code: `reportLine()` in src/core.js.
  - LMP kept: `GA 17+3 Wk by date = U/S at GA 16+3 wk` (at GA = GA by LMP on the U/S day)
  - U/S used: `GA 19+1 Wk by U/S ≠ date at GA 19+1 wk` (at GA = GA from U/S on the U/S day)
  - LMP not remembered (checkbox "จำประจำเดือนไม่ได้" ticked): `GA 11+2 Wk by U/S at GA 11+2 wk due to uncertain date`. Ticking disables the LMP field and ignores any LMP typed there; unticking brings it back.
  - LMP simply not entered, box not ticked: `GA 11+2 Wk by U/S at GA 11+2 wk`
  - LMP only, no U/S yet: `GA 17+2 Wk by date`
  - Doctor override against the rule flips the sign: `by date ≠ U/S` or `by U/S = date`.
  - LMP cannot date the pregnancy (see Clinical rules): the U/S line, e.g. `GA 18+3 Wk by U/S ≠ date at GA 12+0 wk`. The user chose (2026-09-30) to keep the note OUT of this line: it shows in the verdict box with both dates, in the source label under the EDC, and as its own line in "คัดลอกสรุป".
- Red part (GA on the visit date) moves every day in "today" mode; the purple dating part never changes. No line when the visit is before conception or beyond 44+6 wk.
- The handout writes "at 19+1 wk" in one example and "at GA 9+3 wk" in another; the app always writes "at GA". The handout printed case 2's EDC by U/S once as 19/9/2556; the tests use 19/9/2566, which matches the handout's own report line and the LMP + 280 arithmetic.
- tests/core.test.js checks all five handout cases against `reportLine()`; tests/e2e.py checks the rendered line, the day rollover, override and copy. Both files also cover the LMP-conflict cases (the phone case, one day before LMP, a year-early LMP, a 30+0 scan, and a research file with a conflict row).

## EDC from the ANC book (block "EDC ในสมุดฝากครรภ์", requested 2026-09-30)
- A second way for the obstetrician to confirm GA: the EDC written in the ANC book gives GA on the visit date, which moves with "today" like everything else.
- Cross-check only. It never changes the EDC in use, the wheel, or the report line. It says "ตรงกัน" or "ต่างกัน N วัน" against the EDC in use, and explains the gap when it can: the book equals EDC by LMP (maybe not corrected after U/S), equals EDC by U/S, or equals Naegele's calendar rule (+7 days, minus 3 months, plus 1 year) while the app uses LMP + 280.
- Code: `naegeleEDC()` and `checkBookEDC()` in src/core.js. Tests: the screenshot case (LMP 06/08/2569, U/S 01/09/2569 11+0, book 13/05/2570 vs 23/03/2570) in both test files.

## Behaviour that must not regress
- Leap years: dates are converted to C.E. before any arithmetic, so B.E. 2567 (2024) counts 29 Feb although 2567 is not divisible by 4; 29/02/2566 is rejected; 2100 (B.E. 2643) is not leap. Guarded by the fixture test.
- "Today" follows the device clock. Re-checked every 30 s, on visibilitychange, focus, pageshow, and by a timer at local midnight. In "today" mode the visit date moves with it; a typed visit date stays fixed. Focusing the visit field without typing must not switch it to fixed. Covered by tests/e2e.py (clock 30 Sep 2569 23:58 rolls to 1 Oct).
- Dates are integer day numbers, no time zones. Input accepts B.E. and C.E., Thai month names, Thai digits, 8 digits (01092563), Excel serials, and B.E. years typed into a non-Thai Excel (year 2563 stored as C.E.). Two-digit years mean B.E. unless changed in the research settings.
- Research files are read and written in the browser only. Output .xlsx keeps the original sheet and adds columns to the right; .csv uses ISO dates (Stata: date(var, "YMD")).

## Security (reviewed 2026-09-30)
- index.html carries a Content-Security-Policy meta written by build.py: `default-src 'none'`, scripts only by SHA-256 hash of the three inline blocks, the style block by hash, fonts from 'self', `connect-src 'none'` (nothing can be sent out), and `base-uri`, `form-action`, `object-src` 'none'. Any change in src/ changes the hashes, so always rebuild with build.py. A hand edit of index.html silently breaks the page.
- No inline `style="..."` attributes and no inline event handlers anywhere, because the policy blocks them. Use classes and addEventListener.
- dist/artifact.html has no policy of its own (the claude.ai viewer applies one) and still loads Google Fonts and SheetJS from cdnjs.
- The standalone page makes no network request at all. tests/e2e.py section 4 serves it over http and asserts: no external request, no policy violation in normal use (incl. Excel read and both downloads), self-hosted font loads, and fetch() to another site is blocked. Fonts also load when index.html is opened from disk (file://, Chromium checked).
- Live check after commit ec76e0e (2026-09-30, app's browser pane): policy present, every request went to niratorn.github.io only (7 font files), SheetJS 0.18.5 runs under the policy (synthetic .xlsx read in the research tab), example report line correct, no violation until a deliberate fetch() to another site, which was blocked.
- Parsers refuse text longer than `MAX_INPUT_CHARS` (60) so a pathological cell cannot stall the regexes.
- Nothing secret in the repo or its history; commits are authored by Claude noreply. Live headers: HTTPS with HSTS from GitHub Pages; GitHub Pages cannot send frame-ancestors or X-Frame-Options (low impact, no account actions on the page).
- Account notes for the owner: the Claude app's browser pane stays signed in to GitHub; the Claude GitHub connection can push to every repo; use the GitHub noreply email once Git is installed.

## Known limits
- The ANC-book hint "ตรงกับการนับแบบ Naegele ... ส่วนระบบใช้ LMP + 280 วัน" (app.js renderBook) also shows when the EDC in use came from U/S, where the real gap is the U/S redate. Found in review 2026-09-30, not changed.
- SheetJS 0.18.5 is the newest version on npm and cdnjs. It has 2 advisories that apply only to crafted files: GHSA-4r6h-8v6p-xvw6 (prototype pollution, fixed 0.19.3) and GHSA-5pgg-2g8v-p4x9 (ReDoS, fixed 0.20.2). Newer builds are only on cdn.sheetjs.com, which neither the cloud sandbox nor the device shell can reach, so the upgrade waits for the user to download xlsx.full.min.js 0.20.3 into `vendor/`; then point `XLSX_LOCAL` in build.py at it and rebuild. Risk is low because the files are the user's own exports processed locally, and the Content-Security-Policy stops any data from leaving the page.
- The disclaimer box (`div.disclaimer` in src/body.html) holds two bullets in the user's words. The first says it is a calculation aid whose results support the physician's decision. The second asks to check the dates entered every time and says the system stores no personal data. Keep the box. On 2026-09-30 the user removed "ไม่ใช่เครื่องมือแพทย์และยังไม่ผ่านการรับรอง" after being shown that the project playbook asks to keep the not-a-medical-device wording. Do not put it back unless the user asks. README.md still says "ไม่ใช่เครื่องมือแพทย์".
- The public page keeps `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow">`, which is what keeps it out of search results. A robots.txt only counts at the domain root (niratorn.github.io/robots.txt), so one inside this project would do nothing. The public repo page on github.com itself can still be indexed.
